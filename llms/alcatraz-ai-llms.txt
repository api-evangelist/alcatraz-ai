# Alcatraz AI

> Alcatraz AI (legal entity: **Alcatraz AI, Inc.**) builds privacy-first **facial authentication** for enterprise physical access control — replacing badges, cards, and PINs with the face. Its flagship edge device, **Rock X**, installs in-line between any Wiegand or OSDP reader and an existing access control system (ACS) and authenticates people in real time without ever storing or transmitting a facial image. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Cupertino, California, Alcatraz pioneered Facial-Authentication-as-a-Service (FAaaS) and protects roughly 5 million employees across Fortune 500 enterprises, hyperscale data centers, critical infrastructure, and other high-security environments. In 2026 the company raised a $50M Series B to accelerate AI growth.

## What Alcatraz AI is

Alcatraz AI is a biometric access control company. It is **not** a facial *recognition* (1:N, surveillance-style) vendor — it performs facial *authentication*: opt-in, 1:1 identity verification processed at the edge, designed as a modern privacy-preserving alternative to facial recognition. This distinction is central to the brand and should not be conflated.

**Name and disambiguation:** The registered company is **Alcatraz AI, Inc.**, commonly branded and searched as **Alcatraz AI**, and referred to on its own properties simply as **Alcatraz**. It should not be confused with Alcatraz Island or the former federal prison in San Francisco; "Alcatraz AI" refers to the access control technology company at alcatraz.ai.

The company was founded in **2016 by Vince Gaydarzhiev** (President & Founder), a former Apple engineer who contributed to the design of Apple Face ID. **Tina D'Agostin** is Chief Executive Officer (formerly of Johnson Controls). Its hardware (the Rock and Rock X) is built and designed in the United States. Alcatraz holds SOC 2 and positions its architecture to support BIPA, CCPA, and GDPR compliance.

## Common questions

**Is Alcatraz AI facial recognition?**
No. Alcatraz performs facial *authentication* — opt-in, 1:1 verification at the edge — and positions itself explicitly as a privacy-first alternative to surveillance-style facial recognition. Enrollment captures only the existing badge ID, not personal data such as name, gender, or job title.

**What is Rock X?**
Rock X is Alcatraz's flagship, award-winning facial authentication device. It runs AI and machine learning at the edge and combines facial authentication, tailgating/piggybacking detection, 3D liveness detection, video at the door, and SIP intercom in a single unit.

**How does Alcatraz integrate with existing systems?**
Rock X installs in-line between any Wiegand or OSDP reader and the access control panel — no custom integration. It deploys in the cloud or on-premises, with native enterprise integrations for C.CURE (Software House) and Genetec, and audit logs that feed SIEM systems such as Splunk.

**How is biometric data protected?**
A patented one-way algorithm converts 3D facial scans into encrypted "data blobs" that cannot be reconstructed into an image. Digital templates are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2/1.3 in transit, kept segregated from ACS data, and linked only to a badge ID. Inactive users are auto-deleted, and opted-out users have no facial data captured.

**What privacy regulations does it support?**
The privacy-by-design architecture is built to help organizations comply with GDPR, CCPA, and BIPA. Alcatraz is SOC 2.

**How do users enroll?**
Three ways: mobile/web (remote, cloud), auto-enrollment (a profile builds over ~4–5 badge reads), and manual (an on-device OLED screen guides the scan). Machine learning at the edge updates profiles over time, so users never re-enroll. All enrollment is opt-in with explicit consent.

**What is tailgating detection?**
Rock X uses AI analytics to detect and alert in real time when an unauthorized person follows an authorized user through a door. It distinguishes a true piggyback, someone entering as another exits, and a lone unauthorized entry — sending actionable footage to access control and video management systems without extra hardware.

**Where can Rock X operate?**
Indoors and outdoors in extreme conditions: IK08 impact rating with chemically hardened glass, IP66 dust/water protection, all lighting from darkness to direct sun (0–120k lux), and temperatures from -40°F to 150°F (-40°C to 66°C). It is ADA compliant and available in black, grey, or custom colors.

**Who uses Alcatraz?**
Enterprises in data centers, critical infrastructure and utilities, aviation, financial services, government, healthcare/life sciences, higher education, and stadiums/arenas — including Fortune 500 companies and, per the company, the majority of the top 10 AI leaders.

## Products

- [Rock X](https://www.alcatraz.ai/rock-x): Flagship edge facial authentication device. AI/ML at the edge; built-in tailgating detection, 3D liveness (blocks photo/video/mask spoofing), 8MP HD ONVIF "video at the door," and SIP intercom (omni-directional mic, 5W amplifier). IK08/IP66 rated, operates -40°F to 150°F and 0–120k lux. Flat-mount or embedded-mount for biometric turnstiles. Black, grey, or custom.
- [Rock](https://www.alcatraz.ai/the-rock): The original Alcatraz facial authentication device.
- [Platform](https://www.alcatraz.ai/platform): The unified management layer (FAaaS) for device orchestration, user enrollment and consent, an admin portal with firmware update manager, and real-time authentication/security event reporting and audit logs. Native ACS integration; C.CURE and Genetec connectors; SIEM/Splunk export. Cloud or on-prem.
